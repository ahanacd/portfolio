Books I’d press into your hands

How minds work

  • Thinking, Fast and Slow by Daniel Kahneman
    Two ways of thinking, and why the fast one fools us.
  • How Emotions Are Made by Lisa Feldman Barrett
    A case that emotions are built by the brain, not simply triggered.
  • Emotional Intelligence by Daniel Goleman
    Where my own essay on the topic started.
  • The Man Who Mistook His Wife for a Hat by Oliver Sacks
    Case studies of what happens when a brain works differently.

Jealousy, love and other mistakes

  • Othello by William Shakespeare
    The classic study of jealousy left unchecked.
  • Chokher Bali by Rabindranath Tagore
    Desire and envy inside a household, by the founder of Visva-Bharati.
  • Normal People by Sally Rooney
    Two people who struggle to say what they feel.
  • To Live by Yu Hua
    A Chinese novel of survival, read here in translation.

For makers

  • The Design of Everyday Things by Don Norman
    Why good design feels invisible and bad design blames you.
  • How to Lie with Statistics by Darrell Huff
    A short, sharp guide to misleading charts.
